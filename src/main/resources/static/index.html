The PataSure journey

Discover how it works.

A more informed second-hand purchase starts with a product's recorded story. Here's how PataSure connects sellers, products and buyers.

01

A product is registered

An authorised seller registers a second-hand product, providing its identifying details, condition and available description.

02

A unique identity is created

PataSure assigns a unique product identity and generates a QR code linking to its public product page.

03

Its story is recorded

Authorised sellers can add relevant product history, such as repairs, disclosures and ownership-related updates.

04

The buyer scans and explores

A prospective buyer scans the QR code to view available details and recorded history before making a purchase decision.

05

Record integrity is checked

PataSure uses a hash-linked event history to help detect whether previously recorded events have been altered.

Transparency matters. So do honest limitations.

An intact history confirms the integrity of recorded events; it does not independently prove a product's authenticity, condition or legal ownership. Buyers should still inspect products and assess the information provided.

Why PataSure

Second-hand trade deserves more transparency.

Buying a pre-owned product should not mean buying without information. Yet important details about a product's past can be difficult to access, communicate or keep together.

PataSure provides a shared place for product identification, recorded disclosures and history. Our goal is simple: help buyers ask better questions and make more informed decisions.
